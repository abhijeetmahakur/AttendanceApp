Import Students from CSV

Upload a roster CSV (e.g. a Google Form "Student Information Form" export). Expected columns: a full-name column, a plain-integer roll-number column, and a 9-character registration-number column - headers are matched flexibly, so slightly different wording is fine.

  • A row whose registration number already belongs to an existing student updates that student's roll number / registration number only - their login, password, and email are never touched.
  • A row for someone new creates an unclaimed roster entry with no password yet - the student signs themselves up from the Sign Up page, and a teacher must approve it.
  • Rows with no name and no registration number, or missing a registration number, are skipped and listed after the import so they can be fixed and added manually.
